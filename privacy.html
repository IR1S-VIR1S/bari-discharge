Bari Discharge: privacy

Bari Discharge is used only by invited staff of Bari Hospital, 73-G, Gulberg III, Near Firdous Market, Lahore, to write discharge summaries.

Patient records are kept in the hospital's own Google Sheet. On a staff phone they are stored encrypted with that person's PIN until sent. Nothing is shared with any third party, and this website itself stores no patient data.
